# Security policy

Please report security problems **privately**, not in a public issue.

- Open a private report with GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** (Security tab of this repository), or
- email **security@brainitive.app**.

Include the app version (Help → Copy Diagnostics), your OS, and steps to reproduce. We will
acknowledge your report and keep you informed until it is fixed. Never post tokens, cookies or
server addresses you do not want public.

The threat model, what remote content may do and how tokens are stored are in
[docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md).
